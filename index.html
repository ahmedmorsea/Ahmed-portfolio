I'm a video editor and motion storyteller with 4+ years of experience crafting cinematic content for brands across automotive, commercial, and destination verticals. My path started at Pioneers Now, continued at IEA — where I first began working with AI-driven production — and has grown over the past two years at SKOON.

Working mainly in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects, I take raw and AI-generated footage alike and shape it into polished, broadcast-ready films — pacing, color, sound design, and final delivery.

I've collaborated closely with Director Hossam El-Gohary on productions including Sylvia and the Jeep® campaign, alongside AI-driven work for DFSK, AlUla, and other regional and global brands.
